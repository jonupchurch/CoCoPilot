Design system · v0.1

Locked from 5a. Everything here is sized for a panel that lives beside an editor: dense, quiet at rest, and legible over whatever theme is behind it. One panel with subdivisions and a multi-panel arrangement are both covered by the same tokens.

Rules

Do
One status chip per window, always top right.
Mono for anything read from the codebase — paths, symbols, output.
Separate sections with a rule and a caps label, not with a card inside a card.
Keep the resting panel colourless: teal appears on the one thing to press.
Don't
Don't use ember for anything that isn't blocked or failing.
Don't put two signal colours in one control.
Don't shadow anything inside the panel; depth is a border.
Don't use Caprasimo in the product. It is the marketing voice only.
